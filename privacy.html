Privacy Policy

Application: Toddler Sleep Assistant | Last updated: November 3, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Toddler Sleep Assistant. We highly value your trust and are fully committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your children.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Toddler Sleep Assistant does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal or sensitive user data.

The application operates completely offline. All customizable options (such as timers, screen brightness, sound selections, and light preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device via standard local storage mechanisms. We have no access to these settings.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The application contains zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), no telemetry, and no tracking libraries. We do not track user behavior, device telemetry, diagnostics, or crash logs through external platforms.

3. Advertisements and In-App Purchases

The application is 100% ad-free. It does not integrate with any advertising networks, third-party trackers, or monetization partners. There are no in-app purchases or hidden fees.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Because the app is designed for families and young children, we adhere strictly to the guidelines set out by Google Play'
